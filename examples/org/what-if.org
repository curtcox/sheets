* What to try

Annual customers pay once, up front, and stay all year. Monthly customers pay as
they go, and some leave every month. With two months free, monthly billing earns
more in year one at 3% monthly churn, and the annual plan earns more at 3.5%.
Below the crossover the discount costs more than churn does. Clear the checkbox
to see what the discount gives away, and change the price or launch customers to
put dollars on the answer.
