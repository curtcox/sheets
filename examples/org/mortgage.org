* What to try

- Move the down payment to 20%: mortgage insurance disappears.
- Choose a 15-year term: the payment rises by about $970 a month, and the total
  interest falls by more than half.
- Clear the checkbox to see the payment without property tax and home
  insurance, the way many rate quotes show it.

The tax rate, insurance premium, mortgage-insurance rate and the 28% payment share
are fixed assumptions in the second =#+CONSTANTS:= line, listed under *File
constants*. Edit them there for your area and lender.
