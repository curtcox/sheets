#+TITLE: CSV budget to working table

* Imported budget

These values came from CSV. The total formula was added deliberately in Sheets.

| Item       | Quantity | Unit cost | Total |
|------------+----------+-----------+-------|
| Notebooks  |       12 |         4 |     0 |
| Pencils    |       24 |         1 |     0 |
| Folders    |        8 |         3 |     0 |
#+TBLFM: $4=$2*$3

* Try it

Choose *Insert*, paste the CSV below, and inspect the preview. Choose comma as
the separator and keep the first row as a header. Insert the editable table.
Then add a TBLFM line yourself to calculate with the imported values.

#+BEGIN_EXAMPLE
Item,Quantity,Unit cost
Notebooks,12,4
Pencils,24,1
Folders,8,3
#+END_EXAMPLE

Insertion preserves values. It does not import spreadsheet formulas or connections.
