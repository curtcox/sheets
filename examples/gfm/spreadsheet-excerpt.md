## Try it

Copy a rectangular cell range from Excel or Google Sheets, then paste into the source editor. Review the preview and choose **Editable table** or **Picture**. A single copied row also works.

Rich clipboard tables are read as values; scripts and formatting are discarded. For merged cells, copy a rectangular range without merges or paste a screenshot instead. If the clipboard includes an image, **Picture** uses it to preserve appearance.

You can always use **Insert** and choose a CSV file or paste tab-separated values. Spreadsheet formulas and live connections are not imported.
